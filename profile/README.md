We are a software studio from Prague. We build web, iOS and Android apps for businesses, connect them with Slack, Shoptet and the other services a company already runs on, and make browser extensions for Chrome and Safari. Two partners lead the studio and work with a trusted network of specialists.

### What we do

- **Web and mobile apps** for web, iOS and Android, from internal tools to public products
- **Integrations** with Slack, Shoptet, Google Workspace and other services
- **Browser extensions** for Chrome and Safari
- **AI in companies**: agentic workflows and training for Claude Code and Codex
- **Taking over development** from a previous vendor, starting with a free review
- **Hosting** for static websites with git behind the scenes, including the domain and company e-mail

### Selected work

**[Web Editor](https://webeditor.click)** — point at an element of a web page, say what should change, and hand a precise brief to your coding agent. A browser for iPhone and an extension for Chromium. In development, testing by invitation.

**[Signal Collector](https://signalcollector.app)** — an Android app for measuring phone sensors, GNSS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular signal and connection quality. Works offline, published on Google Play.

**[Pásek](https://indigostudio.cz/projekty/pasek)** — a digital passport for old houses, made with an iPhone: floor plan, measurements and a 3D model to hand to an architect. Our own MVP, one week from the first line of code to App Store approval. It did not work out as a product, so we stopped it just as quickly.

More case studies and references: [indigostudio.cz/projekty](https://indigostudio.cz/projekty)
